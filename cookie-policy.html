Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

The short version

We only store what the Patreon login needs to work. We don't use cookies for analytics or advertising on the games page. Some content we embed from other sites may set its own cookies.

What we store

These are all needed for the site to work, so they don't need your consent:

Login pass · raspberrysoft-web-pass (browser storage on this site)

Stored only after you log in with Patreon. Holds your Patreon name and tier so the games page knows which downloads to show. Works for 7 days; after that the games page deletes it on your next visit. Log out deletes it right away.

Notice closed · raspberrysoft-notice-ok (browser storage on this site)

Remembers that you closed the cookie notice, so it doesn't show again.

Login check · web_oauth_state (cookie on our login server, falleneros-auth.onrender.com)

Set when you click Login with Patreon and deleted when you come back, after 10 minutes at most. It makes sure the login that returns is the one you started.

Cookies from other sites

These are set by other companies under their own policies, not by us:

  • Patreon: when you log in on patreon.com.
  • Steam: the "Wishlist on Steam" box on the games and home pages.
  • itch.io and X (Twitter): the embeds on the home page.

How to control them

You can block or delete cookies and site data in your browser's settings. If you block storage for this site, the login won't be remembered between visits. To remove the login pass right away, click Log out on the games page.
