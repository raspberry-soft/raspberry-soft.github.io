Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This page explains how this website uses cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser, so it can remember things between pages and visits.

2. How We Use Cookies

We only use essential cookies, which the website needs to work properly, for example to keep you logged in with Patreon. We don't use cookies for advertising or tracking.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some pages show content from other websites, such as Patreon or Steam. Those websites may set their own cookies, under their own policies.

4. Cookie Consent

Essential cookies are needed for the website to work. By continuing to use the website, you accept their use.

5. Managing Cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. If you do, logging in with Patreon may not work.

6. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
