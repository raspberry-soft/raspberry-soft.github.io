Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This website is run by Fallen Eros / RaspberrySoft, an independent developer of visual novels. This page explains what information we get when you use it and what we do with it.

1. Information We Collect

When you log in with Patreon, Patreon shares basic information from your account with us, such as your name and your membership tier. We never see your Patreon password or payment details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Only to check which downloads your membership includes and show them to you. We don't sell your information, share it, or use it for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services

We use Patreon for login and memberships. See Patreon's Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We don't keep your information on our servers. Your login is saved only in your browser, so you stay logged in for up to 7 days. You can log out at any time, and you can remove our website's access in your Patreon settings.

5. Security

You sign in on Patreon's own website, and this site uses secure (HTTPS) connections.

6. Age Restriction

This website is for adults (18+) only. By using our website, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old (or the applicable legal age in your country) and understand that adult content may be accessed.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
