Lustful Wife Aiko - Synopsis

Lustful Wife Aiko is an adult Visual Novel about Cuckolding. As Hitoshi begins to suspect his wife Aiko's infidelity with her boss, those doubts build into forbidden desires. Will adopting the cuckold lifestyle destroy him?

Hitoshi's boring life suddenly changes when he hears a rumor about his beloved wife Aiko's infidelity with her boss, Ryou, the future heir to the company and his old-time friend.
